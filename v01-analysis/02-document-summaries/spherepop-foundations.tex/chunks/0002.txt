
Let $\mathcal{A}$ denote the amplitwistor scattering operator acting
on complex loci. Then pop induces an operator
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{pop}}:Z_S\to Z_S',
\end{equation}
which modifies complex structure by holomorphic deformation. In
local coordinates this corresponds to insertion of an operator
$\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{pop}}$ acting on a holomorphic differential
form on $PT$.


Contour Deformation


Scattering amplitudes are computed by integrating holomorphic forms
over contours in amplitwistor space. Collapse induces deformation of
these contours, thus modifying amplitudes:
\begin{equation}
\int_\Gamma \Omega
 \quad\mapsto\quad
\int_{\Gamma'} \Omega.
\end{equation}
Hence collapse corresponds to contour modification in twistor space,
in analogy with the deformation of scattering contours in QFT.


Merge as Composite Scattering


Merge $\diamond$ combines geometric regions in $P$; in amplitwistor
geometry this becomes gluing of the corresponding complex loci:
\begin{equation}
Z_{t_1\diamond t_2}
   = \mathrm{Glue}(Z_{t_1},Z_{t_2}).
\end{equation}
If collapse corresponds to scattering, merge corresponds to composition
of scattering processes. A composite pop sequence represents a
multi-vertex twistor interaction.


Holomorphic Action Functional


Let $\Omega$ be the holomorphic volume form on amplitwistor space.
Define the action
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{S}[Z_S]
 = \int_{Z_S}\Omega.
\end{equation}
Collapse transforms $Z_S$ and thus changes $\mathcal{S}$. The result
is a twistor scattering amplitude for collapse. This gives
Spherepop an amplitwistor action principle.


Summary


Collapse in $P$ induces scattering in $PT$: pop acts as a holomorphic
operator, merge corresponds to composite interactions, and geometric
evaluation becomes contour deformation. Pop is scattering.


Amplitwistor Integration and Global Interactions

\label{sec:ampint}

Having interpreted collapse as a scattering process in amplitwistor
space (Sec.~\ref{sec:twistorsc}), we now integrate these local
scattering events to obtain global computational behavior. In
traditional scattering theory, amplitudes are obtained by integrating
local interaction data over contours in a complex manifold. The
analogous construction in {\sc spc} integrates collapse histories
over amplitwistor space to produce global geometric inference.


Collapse Histories


Let $\gamma$ be a collapse history for a term $t$. Under the
amplitwistor representation of Sec.~\ref{sec:ampli}, $\gamma$ maps to
a family of complex loci $\{Z_{\gamma(s)}\}_{s\in[0,1]}$. Collapse
generates a trajectory in amplitwistor space, and global computation
corresponds to integrating holomorphic data along this trajectory.


Global Amplitwistor Integral


Let $\Omega$ be the holomorphic measure in amplitwistor space and let
$Z_{\gamma}$ denote the twistor locus associated with collapse history
$\gamma$. Define the global computation
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{I}(t)
 = \int_{Z_\gamma}\Omega,
\end{equation}
where the integral runs over all collapse-induced loci of $t$.
Hence evaluation of a term in {\sc spc} corresponds to a holomorphic
integral in amplitwistor space.


Nonlocality


Unlike conventional symbolic evaluation, the integral
$\mathcal{I}(t)$ depends on the global geometry of $Z_{\gamma}$. This
encodes nonlocal correlations, as collapse of a local region in $P$
may propagate along null directions in $PT$. In this sense,
computation in the amplitwistor representation is inherently
nonlocal, independent of any explicit message passing mechanism.


Composite Interaction


Merge composes two regions in $P$ into a single computational unit.
Under amplitwistor integration this becomes contour gluing:
\begin{equation}
\int_{Z_{\gamma_1\diamond\gamma_2}}\Omega
 =
\int_{Z_{\gamma_1}}\Omega
  \circ
\int_{Z_{\gamma_2}}\Omega.
\end{equation}
Thus the algebra of merge operations corresponds to the algebra of
composite contour integrals, making merge a global interaction rule.


Scattering Amplitudes as Computation


The integral $\mathcal{I}(t)$ resembles a scattering amplitude
obtained by integrating interaction data over complex contours in
amplitwistor space. In {\sc spc}, collapse produces such interaction
data; global computation is therefore equivalent to evaluating a
scattering amplitude. Pop computes by scattering.


Holomorphic Inference


The global amplitwistor integral provides a principle of inference:
\begin{equation}
t_1 = t_2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal{I}(t_1)=\mathcal{I}(t_2).
\end{equation}
Two terms are equivalent if their amplitwistor integrals coincide. In
contrast to Tensor Logic, which relies on algebraic equalities of
linear operators, {\sc spc} inherits a holomorphic criterion for
computational equivalence.


Summary


Global computation in Spherepop is the amplitwistor integral of
collapse histories. Merge becomes global gluing, collapse becomes
scattering, and equivalence is determined by equality of holomorphic
integrals. Inference is holomorphic, not linear-algebraic.


Twistor--RSVP Coupling

\label{sec:twistrsvp}

The amplitwistor interpretation of Spherepop provides a complex
manifold representation of collapse. The RSVP framework introduces
a scalar field $\Phi$, a vector field $v$, and an entropy field $S$
on the plenum $P$. We now couple these representations by lifting
RSVP fields to twistor space and interpreting collapse as a coupled
holomorphic--thermodynamic evolution. This yields a twistorized form
of the plenum in which computation is expressed through amplitwistor
geometry.


RSVP Fields


Let $P$ be the plenum manifold and $(\Phi,v,S)$ the RSVP fields. The
scalar field $\Phi$ encodes potential, the vector field $v$ encodes
flow, and the entropy field $S$ encodes thermodynamic structure.
These fields evolve according to plenum dynamics, which are not
assumed to arise from a metric variation or from Einstein equations.
The plenum $P$ carries collapse-induced dynamics determined by
thermodynamic constraints.


Twistor Lift


Let $\pi:PT\to P$ denote the twistor fibration. The RSVP fields admit
a twistor lift:
\begin{equation}
\Phi^{\sharp}
 = \pi^\*\Phi,\qquad
v^{\sharp}
 = \pi^\*v,\qquad
S^{\sharp}
 = \pi^\*S.
\end{equation}
Thus the RSVP fields define holomorphic data on twistor space, making
collapse a holomorphic--thermodynamic evolution.


Coupled Collapse


Collapse modifies boundary geometry $S\subset P$, hence modifies the
lifted RSVP fields:
\begin{equation}
(\Phi^{\sharp},v^{\sharp},S^{\sharp})
 \mapsto
(\Phi^{\sharp}+\Delta\Phi^{\sharp},\,
 v^{\sharp}+\Delta v^{\sharp},\,
 S^{\sharp}+\Delta S^{\sharp}).
\end{equation}
These modifications in $PT$ correspond to transformed thermodynamic
conditions in $P$. Collapse therefore couples amplitwistor geometry to
plenum thermodynamics.


Twistor--Lamphrodynamic Semantics


Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the amplitwistor scattering operator of
Sec.~\ref{sec:twistorsc}. Define the twistor--lamphrodynamic operator
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L} :=
\mathcal{A}
  \circ
(\mathrm{lift\;RSVP}).
\end{equation}
Then the semantic value of $t$ in twistor space is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{J}(t)
 = \mathcal{L}(Z_t),
\end{equation}
where $Z_t$ is the amplitwistor locus for term $t$. Thus {\sc spc}
evaluation becomes a lamphrodynamically constrained scattering
process on twistor space.


Thermodynamic Curvature


The RSVP entropy $S$ modulates curvature in $P$. Under the twistor
lift, $S^{\sharp}$ modulates complex geometry in $PT$, influencing
the holomorphic structure of collapse. This yields a curvature
representation coupling thermodynamic and complex-analytic data. The
result is a twistor-thermodynamic geometry that governs computation.


Comparison with Tensor Logic


Tensor Logic encodes computational transformations by linear
operators on embedding vectors. Twistor--RSVP coupling encodes
computational transformations by thermodynamically constrained
holomorphic operators. Tensor Logic discards both thermodynamics and
geometry, replacing collapse by linear algebra. Twistor--RSVP
restores the full geometric--thermodynamic semantics of computation.


Summary


Collapse in Spherepop induces thermodynamic evolution of RSVP fields.
Its twistor representation induces holomorphic evolution of lifted
RSVP fields in amplitwistor space. Computation therefore acquires a
twistor--thermodynamic semantics in which collapse is a coupled
holomorphic--lamphrodynamic process. Tensor Logic corresponds to the
degenerate, zero-curvature projection of this geometry.


Hierarchical Twistor--RSVP Computation

\label{sec:hier}

The coupling developed in Sec.~\ref{sec:twistrsvp} defines computation
as a thermodynamically constrained holomorphic scattering process in
amplitwistor space. In this section we introduce a hierarchical
structure based on nested spheres, which induces hierarchical
twistor--RSVP evolution. Computation propagates across scales of the
plenum and across layers of amplitwistor geometry.


Nested Spheres and Multiscale Collapse


Let $S\subset S'\subset P$ be nested spheres representing multiscale
regions of the plenum. Under twistorization, these map to nested loci
$Z_S\subset Z_{S'}\subset PT$. Collapse of $S$ modifies $Z_S$ and
subsequently modifies $Z_{S'}$ due to their containment relation. Thus
collapse induces multiscale twistor interactions.


Hierarchical Lamphrodynamic Evolution


The RSVP fields $(\Phi,v,S)$ govern local evolution in $P$.
Hierarchically nested spheres induce hierarchical lamphrodynamic
constraints:
\begin{equation}
(\Phi,v,S)_{S'} 
 \mapsto
(\Phi,v,S)_{S}
 \mapsto
(\Phi,v,S)_{\mathrm{int}},
\end{equation}
where the subscript indicates restriction to nested regions. Collapse
propagates thermodynamic constraints across scales, inducing
hierarchical computational behavior.


Twistor Hierarchy


The twistor lift $\pi^\*$ applies to each level of the hierarchy,
producing a hierarchy in $PT$:
\begin{equation}
Z_{\mathrm{global}}
 \supset
Z_{\mathrm{regional}}
 \supset
Z_{\mathrm{local}}.
\end{equation}
Collapse at one level modifies the lifted entropy and complex
structure at finer or coarser levels. Thus computation is both local
and global in twistor space.


Hierarchical Scattering


Let $\mathcal{A}$ denote the twistor scattering operator. Nested
collapse yields
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{A}_{S}
 \circ
\mathcal{A}_{S'}
 \circ
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{global}},
\end{equation}
representing a chain of twistor interactions. Computation is thus a
hierarchical scattering process governed by RSVP thermodynamics.


Multiscale Integration


The global amplitwistor integral of Sec.~\ref{sec:ampint} decomposes
into nested contributions:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{I}(t)
 =
\int_{Z_{\mathrm{global}}}\Omega
 =
\int_{Z_{\mathrm{regional}}}\Omega
 =
\int_{Z_{\mathrm{local}}}\Omega,
\end{equation}
yielding a multiscale holomorphic inference principle. Local collapse
participates in global inference by way of the twistor hierarchy.


Summary


Nested spheres induce hierarchical lamphrodynamic constraints, which
lift to hierarchical twistor geometry. Collapse propagates across
scales, producing multiscale scattering events and global
holomorphic inference. Computation is therefore hierarchical in both
the plenum and twistor space.


Holomorphic Entropy and Nonlocal Inference

\label{sec:holomentropy}

Entropy in RSVP induces lamphrodynamic relaxation in the plenum. Under
the twistor lift, entropy becomes a holomorphic field in amplitwistor
space. We now show that collapse induces holomorphic entropy flow,
producing nonlocal inference by complex propagation rather than local
message passing or symbolic deduction.

Holomorphic Entropy Field


Let $S^{\sharp}=\pi^\*S$ be the lifted entropy field. Collapse alters
$S^{\sharp}$ along complex loci $Z_S$, inducing a holomorphic entropy
flow
\begin{equation}
\bar{\partial}S^{\sharp}
  = \Delta S^{\sharp}\, .
\end{equation}
This identifies collapse with entropy-driven evolution in twistor
space.

Nonlocality


The modification of $S^{\sharp}$ along $Z_S$ propagates in $PT$ along

This differs fundamentally from Tensor Logic, which relies on local
embedding coordinates and linear contraction.


Holomorphic Inference


Inference between regions $S_A$ and $S_B$ occurs when collapse of
$S_A$ modifies $S^{\sharp}$ at $S_B$. The resulting inference relation
is given by
\begin{equation}
S_A \leadsto S_B
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Delta S^{\sharp}(Z_{S_A})
  \longrightarrow
\Delta S^{\sharp}(Z_{S_B}).
\end{equation}
Thus inference arises through holomorphic entropy flow, not symbolic
deduction.

Complex-Analytic Learning


Learning in {\sc spc} (Sec.~6.5) minimizes plenum energy. Under the
twistor lift, learning corresponds to modifying $S^{\sharp}$ to
reduce holomorphic variation. Collapse (learning) therefore drives
the system toward holomorphic equilibrium states in amplitwistor
space.

Summary


Entropy couples collapse to a holomorphic differential operator,
producing nonlocal inference through complex propagation. Learning
minimizes entropy variation, driving the system toward holomorphic
equilibrium. Inference is holomorphic and nonlocal.

Formal Specification of Spherepop Calculus



Syntax


Let $\mathcal{S}$ be the class of admissible spheres in the plenum $P$.
Spherepop terms are expressions of the form
\[
t ::= S \;\mid\; \mathrm{pop}(t)\;\mid\; t_1\diamond t_2\;\mid\; t[\Phi,v,S],
\]
where $S\in\mathcal{S}$, $\mathrm{pop}(t)$ denotes collapse,
$t_1\diamond t_2$ denotes merge, and $t[\Phi,v,S]$ denotes evaluation
relative to the ambient fields $(\Phi,v,S)$.


Types


Spherepop types are given by
\[
\tau ::= \mathbf{Sphere}\;\mid\;\mathbf{Pop}\;\mid\;\mathbf{Merge}
         \;\mid\;\mathbf{RSVP}.
\]

Typing judgments $\Gamma\vdash t:\tau$ satisfy
\[
\frac{}{\,\Gamma\vdash S:\mathbf{Sphere}\,}, \qquad
\frac{\Gamma\vdash t:\mathbf{Sphere}}
     {\,\Gamma\vdash\mathrm{pop}(t):\mathbf{Pop}\,},
\]
\[
\frac{\Gamma\vdash t_1:\mathbf{Sphere}\qquad\Gamma\vdash t_2:\mathbf{Sphere}}
     {\,\Gamma\vdash t_1\diamond t_2:\mathbf{Merge}\,},
\qquad
\frac{\Gamma\vdash t:\mathbf{Sphere}}
     {\,\Gamma\vdash t[\Phi,v,S]:\mathbf{RSVP}\,}.
\]


Operational Semantics


Let $\llbracket t\rrbracket$ denote the geometric configuration
represented by $t$. Evaluation is defined by
\[
\llbracket S\rrbracket = S,\qquad
\llbracket \mathrm{pop}(t)\rrbracket =
\mathrm{pop}(\llbracket t\rrbracket),
\]
\[
\llbracket t_1\diamond t_2\rrbracket
  =\mathrm{pop}(\llbracket t_1\rrbracket\cup\llbracket t_2\rrbracket),
\qquad
\llbracket t[\Phi,v,S]\rrbracket = (t,\Phi,v,S).
\]
Evaluation yields geometric configurations rather than symbolic
values.


Normal Forms


A term $t$ is in normal form if $\mathrm{pop}(t)=t$ up to homotopy.
Confluence and adequacy (proved in the main text) imply that normal
forms are unique up to geometric equivalence.


RSVP Fields and Variational Formulation


Let $P$ be a differentiable manifold (the plenum). An RSVP structure is
specified by fields
\[
(\Phi,\,v,\,S),
\]
where $\Phi$ is a scalar potential, $v$ a vector field (interpreted as
flow), and $S$ an entropy field. Collapse and merge operations interact
with these fields through a variational principle. A standard
Lagrangian density is given by
\[
\mathcal{L}[\Phi,v,S]
 = \tfrac{1}{2}\,|\nabla\Phi|^2
   +\langle v,\nabla\Phi\rangle
   -S,
\]
where $|\nabla\Phi|^2$ denotes the metric contraction of the gradient,
and $\langle v,\nabla\Phi\rangle$ denotes flow-mediated interaction.
The entropy term $-S$ represents internal-to-boundary dissipation.

Euler--Lagrange variation yields a coupled PDE system of the form
\[
\Delta\Phi - \mathrm{div}(\Phi v)= \partial S/\partial t,
\qquad
\partial_t\,v + (v\cdot\nabla)v = -\nabla\Phi,
\]
\[
\partial_t\,S + \mathrm{div}(S\,v) = \Delta S,
\]
interpreted as potential relaxation, flow advection, and entropy
diffusion respectively. These equations specify lamphrodynamic
constraints on geometric evolution under collapse. Their precise form
may vary with the choice of background metric, constitutive relation,
or thermodynamic model; the above form is representative of the
structure assumed in the main text.


Lamphrodynamic Evolution and Hamiltonian Structure


Lamphrodynamics denotes the thermodynamically constrained evolution of
geometric configurations under collapse and merge in the presence of
the RSVP fields. A Hamiltonian functional capturing internal energy
and entropy release may be written
\[
\mathcal{H}[\Phi,v,S]
  = \int_P
    \Big(
       \tfrac{1}{2}\,|v|^2
       +\tfrac{1}{2}\,|\nabla\Phi|^2
       -S
    \Big)\, d\mathrm{vol},
\]
where the first two terms represent kinetic and potential energy
contributions, and the last term encodes entropy release. Collapse
reduces $\mathcal{H}$ by increasing boundary entropy while decreasing
internal potential.

In the main text, collapse is interpreted as a geometric operation
$D_{\mathrm{pop}}$ acting on spheres in $P$. In Hamiltonian form this
is realized as a vector field on function space,
\[
\dot{\Phi} = -\,\frac{\delta \mathcal{H}}{\delta \Phi}, \qquad
\dot{v} = -\,\frac{\delta \mathcal{H}}{\delta v}, \qquad
\dot{S} =  \frac{\delta \mathcal{H}}{\delta S},
\]
modulated by admissibility conditions reflecting boundary effects and
flow constraints. Collapse thus induces a Hamiltonian evolution
constrained by entropy production and flow compatibility. Under the
amplitwistor lift (Sections~\ref{sec:twistorsc}--\ref{sec:twistrsvp}),
this Hamiltonian vector field becomes a holomorphic differential
operator acting on the corresponding amplitwistor loci, producing a
coupled geometric--thermodynamic evolution in amplitwistor space.
