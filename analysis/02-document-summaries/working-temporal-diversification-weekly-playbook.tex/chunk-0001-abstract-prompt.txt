1    Goal
Sustain engagement by managing a portfolio of unfinished histories on different clocks, not by
demanding continuous novelty from a single process.


2    Weekly Portfolio Construction
Maintain at least four active scopes:

• Fast (τ ≈ minutes–hours): daily execution and immediate feedback.

• Medium (τ ≈ days): experiments and short deliverables.

• Slow (τ ≈ weeks): proofs, architecture shifts, chapter drafts.

• Very slow (τ ≈ months+): foundational programs and long manuscripts.

1. Never require all scopes to POP weekly. Long horizons stay unresolved by design.

4. Use REFUSE to cut dead branches quickly. Remove known-nonproductive continuations.

5. Use COLLAPSE to reduce bookkeeping overhead. Merge distinctions no longer decision-
   relevant.
