1    Purpose
This experiment family operationalizes the bridge between The Geometry of Boredom and Sphere-
pop by modeling boredom as a scope-selection problem over multiple unresolved histories with
heterogeneous temporal horizons.
Remark 1.1 (Disambiguation from Appendix B Plan B). This experiment suite is unrelated to
the repository’s pending Appendix B poset-integration rewrite. It does not change Spherepop’s four
primitives or claim that full Appendix B semantics is already implemented. It introduces a separate
scheduling formalization for multi-timescale continuation using existing vocabulary.


2    Core Formalization
Remark 2.1 (Grounding and status). This document treats EMCG-based scope scheduling as a
new proposed extension, not as an already-established theorem in the existing Spherepop specifi-
cation. A related but distinct saturation notion already exists in this corpus via the Asymptotic
Saturation Theorem in textbook/The Ecology of Distinctions.tex (§“Asymptotic Saturation
Without Exhaustion”), which concerns reintegration dynamics rather than task-portfolio schedul-
ing. The present experiment asks whether an analogous local-vs-field saturation distinction is useful
in practice at the workflow level.
Definition 2.2 (Scope portfolio). A portfolio is
                   T = {(Si , τi )}ni=1 ,   Si = (Oi , τi ),   τ1 ≪ τ2 ≪ · · · ≪ τn ,
where each unresolved scope Si has local option space Oi and horizon τi .
Definition 2.3 (Local and field saturation). With operator Ft = C(Ht ),
                                LocalSat(i, t) : max EMCGi (a | Ft ) ≈ 0,
                                                  a∈Oi

                              FieldSat(t) : max max EMCGi (a | Ft ) ≈ 0.
                                              i    a∈Oi

Proposition 2.4 (Scope-switch recovery). LocalSat(i, t) does not imply FieldSat(t). If another
scope j satisfies maxa∈Oj EMCGj (a | Ft ) > 0, switching scopes restores progress.
Proposition 2.5 (Operator-drift recovery). A scope can transition from EMCGi (a | Ft ) ≈ 0 to
EMCGi (a | Ft+∆t ) > 0 without changing Oi , due to intervening work on other scopes that modifies
F.
Proposition 2.6 (BIND-as-progress). Long-horizon scopes can accumulate useful structure through
BIND/REFUSE/COLLAPSE while remaining unresolved; progress does not require immediate
POP.
