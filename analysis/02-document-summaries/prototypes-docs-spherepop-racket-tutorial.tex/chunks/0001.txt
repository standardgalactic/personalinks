1    Getting Started
Assuming the collection is available on your Racket path, you can require it as follows:

#lang racket
(require spherepop-lib)

   This gives you access to the core region type, merge and collapse, and the tiny sp surface
syntax.


2    First Examples
(define s default-collapse-strategy)
(define a (make-region "a" ’(1)))
(define b (make-region "b" ’(2)))
(define t (sp (a b)))
(define r (eval-term s t))
